# 🍕 Maven Pizza Sales Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes pizza sales data using SQL and Power BI to uncover business insights related to revenue, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and sales trends. The goal is to support data-driven decision-making and identify opportunities for business growth.

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Pizzas Sold
- Average Order Value
- Average Pizzas per Order

## Key Insights
- A small number of pizza varieties generate a significant share of total revenue.
- Sales peak during lunch and dinner hours, indicating clear customer demand patterns.
- Weekend sales outperform weekdays, highlighting opportunities for targeted promotions.
- Certain pizza categories and sizes consistently contribute more to revenue than others.
- Underperforming products can be reviewed for menu optimization.

## Recommendations
- Focus inventory and marketing efforts on top-selling pizzas.
- Introduce bundle offers and upselling strategies to increase average order value.
- Optimize staffing during peak sales hours.
- Run promotional campaigns during low-demand periods to improve sales consistency.

## Dashboard Highlights
- Revenue & Sales KPIs
- Daily and Hourly Sales Trends
- Best & Worst Selling Pizzas
- Category-wise and Size-wise Performance
- Interactive Filters and Visualizations
